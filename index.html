How a tool inside a pipe finds lost metal

Two interactive isometric figures. Drag the tool sideways, or leave it to play its tour. Where the pipe wall is thinner, magnetic flux leaks out of the steel, and a ring of sensors riding the wall reads it.

Pipeline run

The tool is launched from a trap, runs four welded spools under a road and is caught in the receiver. Welds and three kinds of metal loss build up on an eight-row record.
